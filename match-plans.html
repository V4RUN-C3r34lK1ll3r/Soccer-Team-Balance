    ROTATION PLAN

    Fresh legs without losing our shape

    We have a larger squad, so everyone should get meaningful minutes. Rotations happen at calm moments and each substitute receives one clear role before entering.

    • Midfield: Prajna and Sangram rotate. One presses while the other player in midfield protects the center.
    • Goalkeeper: Minti starts; DJ and Shailesh provide planned cover. Change at halftime or between games when possible.
    • Defense: Chirag leads the line. Shailesh, Minti, Rajeev, and DJ rotate around him.
    • Attack: Nuhu and Varun lead; Vivek provides fresh legs and presses when introduced.
    • Special marking: DJ or Varun tracks quick opponents such as Pradnyal or Bhagyesh.
